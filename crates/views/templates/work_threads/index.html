Work threads

A work thread is a channel thread with a status and an owner. This list gathers tracked work from every channel you belong to, so you can pick it back up without searching through archived threads.

How to start a work thread
  1. Open a channel and choose Threads in the channel header.
  2. Open an existing thread, or choose New thread to start one from scratch or from a message.
  3. In the thread, open Manage and choose Track as work. The thread starts as Planned.
  4. Use Update work to assign an owner from the channel and move it through In progress, Blocked, and Done.

The person who started the thread, the channel's creator, or an administrator can turn tracking on and assign an owner. The assigned owner can update the status. Completing work keeps the conversation and its history.
