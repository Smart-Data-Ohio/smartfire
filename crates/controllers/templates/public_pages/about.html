About Smartfire

Smartfire is self-hosted, open-source team chat. Each workspace runs on its own server, operated by the organization that set it up, and that organization controls its workspace's data.

What you can do here

  • Talk in rooms organized by topic or team, with threads for side conversations.
  • Share files, both uploads stored on the workspace server and Google Drive links.
  • Plan with room events, and optionally publish your Going and Maybe RSVPs to your own Google Calendar.
  • Meet over voice and video huddles without leaving the room.
  • Connect optional integrations your workspace enables, such as GitHub pull-request cards, bots, and agents.

Signing in with Google

Where the workspace enables it, members can sign in with their Google Workspace account. Google sign-in only verifies your identity: it does not connect your calendar or your Drive files. Connecting Google Calendar or Drive previews is a separate, optional choice you make later from your profile.

Your data stays with this workspace

Messages you post, including direct messages, and files you upload are stored on this workspace's server and may remain in its backups. People who operate that server can access that data. Room membership still controls what ordinary members can see.
