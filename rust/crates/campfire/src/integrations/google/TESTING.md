The `ca.pem`, `test.pem`, and `test.key` files are disposable local TLS fixtures,
created for this port. They authenticate only the fake Google servers in native
tests. No Google credentials or production keys are present.

The leaf names are `oauth2.googleapis.com` and `www.googleapis.com`. The tests keep
those hosts, port 443, and normal TLS hostname verification; a mapping dialer sends
the connection to a local socket, and only the test CA is trusted there.

OIDC tokens and JWKS come from `vectors/rails_compat_smartfire.json`, generated by
our Rails reference at the pin. OAuth redirects and PKCE/state encodings come from
`vectors/google_sign_in.json` (`reference-tools/google/generate-sign-in.sh`).

Run `python3 reference-tools/google/prove-sign-in.py` from `rust/` to prove state,
ID-token, and email-trust bypasses fail the tests. It restores each mutated source
in `finally` and writes its logs under the worktree's `.scratch/ws14g/mutations/`.
